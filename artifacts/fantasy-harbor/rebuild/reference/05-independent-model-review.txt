1. SILHOUETTE / PROPORTION / COMPOSITION — FIX FIRST

The target's appeal is a rounded youthful face with large open expressive eyes, compact rounded nose, lively smile, sweeping dark-brown hair, soft jacket silhouette and relaxed trousers. The model instead reads as an elongated narrow-eyed adult face on a slim toy body. Its cheek/chin length and pronounced nose profile work against the requested cute expression. The giant cranial mass alone does not produce the target's proportions.
Priority corrections:
- Rebuild the facial masses around large, rounded eye openings and irises, fuller cheeks, shorter lower face, softer jaw and a smaller rounded nose. Establish a warm open smile, not a thin horizontal mouth slit. Review front AND profile before adding detail.
- Replace the flat front fringe slab and repeated parallel hanging rear locks with a unified scalp volume and a few broad, curved, overlapping swept locks. Match the target's rounded silhouette, sideburn transition and volume over the forehead; current rear hair reads like a vertical mop attached high on the skull.
- Give the jacket a coherent raised collar, shoulder-to-sleeve transition, shaped front opening and longer rounded hem. Pale exposed shapes across both shoulders and the neck opening read as intersections or gaps. Inspect and eliminate those artifacts. The forearms emerge abruptly from broad sleeves; extend proper cuffs and make wrist transitions deliberate.
- Relax the trousers into fabric volume at thighs/knees and add clear turned-up ankle cuffs. Current pinched calves and smooth tight legs read as leggings. Round and refine the boot forms and sole transitions rather than keeping slab-like sole outlines.
- Pose hands naturally with shorter, softly tapered digits and a clear palm/wrist silhouette. The current splayed narrow fingers are distracting. Supply a rear view after corrections; none was submitted this round.

BAY
The aerial view exposes the primary mismatch: a flat, mostly empty park island ringed by a uniform wall of rock blocks. Four detached similar cottages and widely spaced small trees do not form the target's layered coastal settlement. The concept has an enclosing water bay, a curving inhabited shore, buildings and terraces stepping upward, a spreading tree canopy, substantial ruined arches at the water edge and distant cliff/lighthouse depth. The arrival view looks into one centered cottage, obscuring the bay instead of revealing its sweep.
Priority corrections:
- Rework the terrain and settlement layout before scattering props. Establish a curved inner water basin/beach, irregular rock shore, terraced village bank and taller distant cliff masses. Avoid a continuous same-height rock fence and a huge empty level lawn.
- Group buildings into a connected, varied village composition: stagger footprints, elevations, roof heights, facade setbacks and routes. Place substantial foreground architecture on one side of the pier view, opening the center toward water and the far shore.
- Make one broad leafy tree an intentional middle-ground anchor; current thin flat umbrella crowns and repeated trunks leave most of the scene empty. Use fuller layered crowns and vegetation clusters with clear scale variation.
- Bring arches into a coherent coastal ruin composition with varied surviving heights, masonry thickness, broken ends and rocky footings. Current small freestanding identical arches at the back read as park ornaments.
- Supply the missing distant lighthouse/clifftop silhouette and a human-scale route through the terraces. Restore foreground/middle/background overlap. Render a concept-comparable arrival camera; the current front-on cottage view is insufficient for that comparison.
